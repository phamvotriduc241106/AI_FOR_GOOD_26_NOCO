GROUP A: MUST ASK (blocks the MUST features; about 3 minutes)
A1. Heating/cooling degree days.
    "In your Buffalo example the heating degree days look like 6,075 and the cooling degree days 650. Are those the values your
     calculator uses for Buffalo?"
    -> affects: Assumptions.hdd / cdd, golden test (T2).
A2. Cooling load.
    "Our reverse-engineering matches your example only if the cooling load applies the operating load factor twice (0.75 x 0.75).
     Is that how the sheet works?"
    -> affects: calc.py cooling formula (T2).
A3. Perimeter.
    "The sheet shows an 'Estimated Perimeter' of 500 ft for a one-floor, very long and narrow, 10,000 sq ft building. How is that
     estimated? Would you accept the real building outline from public map data instead?"
    -> affects: how T3/T2 derive perimeter from the OpenStreetMap polygon.
A4. Installed cost.
    "What installed cost per square foot should we use for wall insulation? A low-to-high range is fine."
    -> affects: Assumptions.cost_per_sqft, payback and net investment (otherwise the app shows 'needs cost').
A5. Incentive.
    "Is the National Grid Commercial Weatherization incentive $4.00 per square foot of insulated wall area? Is there a cap or an
     eligibility rule?"
    -> affects: Assumptions.incentive_per_sqft, report text.
A6. Permission.
    "May we show your calculator screenshot or its numbers in our slides and demo as a test case? May we use AI tools on it?"
    -> affects: slide 4 (T8) and the repo rule on partner data.
