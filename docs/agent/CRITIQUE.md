# Critique of BRIEF.md

Source citations below refer to line numbers in `docs/agent/transcript_snapshot.txt` (T). Brief references use `BRIEF.md` line numbers (B). The brief is largely faithful; distinguish source facts from proposed scope.

## (a) Unsupported or overstated claims

- **“Requesters … are the inputs, not the users” (B22):** Staff are explicitly users, but the source does not exclude requester-facing intake. Treat staff-only interaction as a proposed scope choice (T69, T87, T103).
- **“The system could (all optional)” (B26):** “Could” introduces suggested capabilities, but does not establish formal acceptance criteria or permission to omit every capability. The source separately says the system “should” support the animal's journey (T82–85). Confirm the minimum expected scope.
- **NOCO “Judging criteria: Not stated” (B48):** No complete rubric or weights appear, but explicit “Bonus points” are limited scoring guidance. Say “Full rubric absent; bonus categories stated” (T165–170).
- **Photo-date inference (B52):** Filename digits suggest a date, but do not verify capture time or event date. The brief labels this inference correctly; do not promote it to a scheduling fact (T3, T20, T46).

## (b) Important details omitted or compressed

- **AAO's end-to-end journey:** The explicit “should” statement covers first contact, intake, evaluation, foster/sanctuary placement, veterinary and behavioral care, adoption or other resolution. The brief's feature list loses that overarching requirement (T82–83).
- **AAO's scale and crisis context:** “Dozens” of daily requests include financial/housing crises and attempts to avoid behavioral euthanasia. These explain urgency and why compassionate handling matters (T69).
- **Prior-contact checks and individualized care:** Staff need to determine whether an animal has interacted with AAO before; the goal is helping more animals without sacrificing individualized care. Both inform a useful demo and success measure (T78, T112).
- **Broader knowledge queries:** Medication, trainer advice, prior contact by a person, and safe interactions between sanctuary dogs show the breadth and sensitivity of the knowledge task beyond the brief's bite-history example (T103).

## (c) Possible transcription errors / likely intended wording

- **No supported corrections:** This is a typed transcription of six photos, including one duplicate page, not speech. No audio or original images are available in the reviewed files to verify errors (T3, T72–73).
- Preserve **ACV Auctions**, **Copart**, **Against All Oddz Animal Alliance**, **Shelterluv**, and **NOCO** as written; there is no evidence for alternative spellings (T29, T69, T76, T114).
- Preserve **3–5× greater value** as an aspiration, not 3–5%, dollars, or a demonstrated result (T35). “Dozens” is not an exact daily count (T69).
- No event times appear. Filename strings are image identifiers, not deadlines; six photos correspond to five distinct pages (T3, T20, T46, T72–73, T98, T131).

## (d) Risks for the three-person, one-day team

- **Scope overload:** AAO spans intake through lifelong records; NOCO lists many energy measures; ACV is deliberately open-ended. Humans should select one user and one demonstrable flow before implementation (T35–43, T82–110, T134–146).
- **Unconfirmed dependencies:** Sources and systems are named, but credentials, usable datasets, APIs and access permissions are not supplied. Do not plan around live integrations; prepare synthetic inputs using the existing template (T57, T76, T101, T165–170).
- **Privacy and unsafe advice:** AAO involves personal crises, medical/behavioral records and bite/legal concerns. Keep the demo synthetic, preserve source evidence, and route consequential judgments and drafted replies to staff review (T69, T89, T101–108).
- **Unsupported numbers:** ACV supplies no baseline for 3–5× value; NOCO requests savings, costs, incentives and ROI without formulas, rates or eligibility rules. Label demo assumptions, use deterministic calculations, and have humans confirm inputs and the meaning of “value” (T35, T148–162).
- **False certainty:** Missing, conflicting or outdated records must remain visible; capacity comparisons require current resource information. A confident answer without evidence would undermine AAO's stated needs (T78, T93, T104).
- **Event uncertainty:** These are challenge statements, not event rules. Deadline, submission format, complete judging rubric, challenge assignment and permission to use the prebuilt template remain unconfirmed; NOCO's bonus list is the only explicit scoring guidance here (T1–3, T165–170).
